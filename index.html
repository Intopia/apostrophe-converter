Apostrophe converter

Finds the apostrophes in contractions and possessives, and replaces each one with &#8217;, the numeric entity for a curly apostrophe. It works in HTML and in plain XML, including RSS, SVG and XHTML without a DTD.

Nothing you paste or upload here is sent anywhere. This tool runs entirely in your browser.

Accepts .md, .markdown, .txt, .html, .htm and .xml files. The contents will be added to the text box above.
